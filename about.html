About me

Water resources engineer working where hydraulic modelling, GIS and data analysis meet real water and wastewater infrastructure.

I'm a Design Engineer at Vastushilpi Project and Consultancy in Bhopal, where I work on water supply, wastewater and storm water projects across Madhya Pradesh. My day-to-day includes preparing DPRs and cost estimates for sewage treatment plant rehabilitation and building storm water drainage models in SewerGEMS.

I completed my M.Tech in Water Engineering and Management at IIT Kharagpur in 2026. There I built a Python-integrated EPANET framework for pressure management in water distribution networks, and worked on GIS and remote sensing applications ranging from watershed delineation to estimating suspended solids from Sentinel-2 imagery. Before that, I studied Agricultural Engineering at Mahatma Gandhi Chitrakoot Gramodaya Vishwavidyalaya.

I enjoy pairing engineering judgement with data: a four-year study of the Geonkhali water treatment plant, for example, became an interactive Python and Tableau dashboard that made seasonal and tidal effects easy to see. I'm most interested in water infrastructure planning and sustainable water and wastewater systems.
