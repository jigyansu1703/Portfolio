How a project runs

  1. Share the scope

    Send the town or area, network length and deadline, with whatever data you have: layout, levels, population, existing model.

  2. Fixed quote

    Within 24 hours you get a fixed price, delivery date and list of any missing inputs.

  3. First model run

    I share the model and key results for your review before finalising the design.

  4. Final delivery

    Model file, results tables, longitudinal sections and maps, plus the report chapter if ordered.
