Geonkhali water treatment plant performance study

Research internship at Haldia Water Services, May – June 2025

  • Python
  • Tableau
  • Water quality
  • Filtration

Problem

The Geonkhali WTP draws raw water whose quality swings with tides and with the monsoon. Operators needed a clear picture of how those swings affected treatment performance over time, but the data sat in years of operational logs.

Approach

  • Cleaned and analysed four years of operational water quality records in Python.
  • Compared raw and treated turbidity across monsoon and non-monsoon seasons and tidal conditions.
  • Tracked filtration efficiency to see where treatment performance dipped.
  • Built an interactive Tableau dashboard so the trends could be explored without re-running the analysis.

Outcome

The dashboard made seasonal and tidal effects visible at a glance and sped up routine water quality analysis. The findings were written up in analytical reports to support process optimisation at the plant.
